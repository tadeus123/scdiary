What it is
Buyers stay in ChatGPT. They do not join a new platform.
Airsup is infrastructure that helps ChatGPT find real company domains,
answer from what the factory published, and send a clearer project.
Free for suppliers. Buyers pay.

How a factory becomes findable
1. Enter the company website
2. Confirm with a mailbox on that domain
3. Check scraped facts and add WeChat / sample lead / reply style
4. Publish so ChatGPT can ask you

Success means domain confirmed and published. Filling a form alone is not enough.
